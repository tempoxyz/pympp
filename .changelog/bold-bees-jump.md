pympp: minor
---

Aligned servers and clients with the core spec: rejected credentials report specific problem types, replays get `402` with a fresh `invalid-challenge`, HMAC secrets must be at least 32 bytes, and method IDs must be lowercase letters. Added the client `on_challenge` approval hook. Tempo pull transactions no longer need the attribution memo but reject any attribution memo not bound to their realm and challenge, and servers reject, as mppx does, a pull transaction that is already on chain before their own broadcast, reporting it as a replay, while the retry of a pull they broadcast themselves whose outcome was lost settles; a receipt found after a failed first send settles only with an attribution memo bound to its challenge. Tempo push (hash) credentials still require the memo bound to their challenge. Servers reject invalid method IDs at startup, and clients, including the MCP client, never pay again automatically after a paid retry is answered with a new Challenge.
